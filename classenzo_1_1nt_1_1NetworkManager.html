Detailed Description

The central coordinator of the engine's node system.

The network manager is the central coordinator of the engine’s node system. It manages the lifecycle of nodes, including their creation, storage, and validation, while also tracking dependencies between them. Acting as a singleton, it ensures that all parts of the engine work with a single consistent view of the network, providing global access. Beyond just storing nodes, it also controls cooking and traversing dependency graphs, ensuring that updates flow correctly through the network when nodes change.
